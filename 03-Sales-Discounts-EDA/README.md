# 📊 Assignment 03 - Sales & Discounts EDA

## 📌 Overview

This assignment focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Sales & Discounts dataset. Descriptive statistics and data visualization techniques are used to understand numerical and categorical variables, identify distributions, and detect potential outliers.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Perform descriptive statistical analysis.
- Understand the distribution of numerical variables.
- Identify potential outliers using box plots.
- Analyze categorical variables using bar charts.
- Interpret patterns and trends in the dataset.
- Summarize important findings from the analysis.

## 📊 Dataset

The analysis is performed on a Sales & Discounts dataset containing numerical and categorical sales-related variables.

Key numerical variables include:

- Volume
- Avg Price
- Total Sales Value
- Discount Rate (%)
- Discount Amount
- Net Sales Value

Categorical variables include:

- Business Unit (BU)
- Day
- Brand
- City
- SKU
- Model

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Descriptive Statistics

Calculated:

- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard Deviation

These measures were used to understand the central tendency and variation of the numerical variables.

### 2. Histograms

Histograms were created for the numerical variables to understand their distributions.

The analysis showed that several numerical variables are positively skewed, with a small number of high-value observations.

### 3. Box Plots

Box plots were used to identify potential outliers.

Potential high-value outliers were observed in variables such as:

- Avg Price
- Total Sales Value
- Discount Amount
- Net Sales Value

### 4. Categorical Analysis

Bar charts were used to analyze categorical variables.

The Business Units were approximately evenly represented, while some brands appeared more frequently than others.
